## What AGPL means for you

You may use, study, modify and redistribute this code. In exchange:

1. **Publish your source.** If you run a modified version on a public server,
   you must offer its complete source to that server's users. This is the
   clause that separates AGPL from plain GPL — running it as a service counts
   as distribution.
2. **Keep the same license.** Derivative works stay AGPL-3.0-or-later.
3. **Credit the origin.** Keep the copyright notices, and link back to
   <https://github.com/goudarz/tuxly>.

A visible "based on Tuxly" link in your site footer, pointing at the upstream
repository and at your own source, satisfies both (1) and (3) in practice.

Content is separate: reuse the articles under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution
and share-alike. Articles sourced from elsewhere keep their original license,
noted at the foot of each page.
